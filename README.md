## Abstract

The Hospital Emergency-Room Triage System is designed to help prioritize patients based on the severity of their medical condition. The system collects basic patient information and emergency details, assigns a priority level, and helps medical staff identify which patients require immediate attention.

The project aims to organize the emergency-room patient queue efficiently and reduce delays in attending to critical cases.

## Project Objective

The main objectives of the system are:

* To collect basic patient emergency information.
* To assess and categorize patients according to predefined triage criteria.
* To assign appropriate priority levels.
* To organize patients based on their priority.
* To help emergency-room staff manage the patient queue efficiently.
